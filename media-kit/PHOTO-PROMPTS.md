# Example photo prompts

The four photos were generated with the built-in image generation tool in
`photorealistic-natural` mode. All people and situations are fictional.

## Bench materials test

An editorial 16:9 photograph of a fictional materials researcher running a
bench-top falsification test on two metal sensor housings. Include calipers,
temperature probes, colored-dot sample trays, and a laptop showing an abstract
evidence graph with no legible text. Use soft morning window light, charcoal,
muted blue, wood, amber, and teal. No logos, trademarks, watermark, medical
setting, futuristic holograms, or staged stock-photo smile.

## Field water sampling

An editorial 16:9 photograph of a fictional environmental field researcher
collecting creek-water samples while recording observations on a rugged offline
laptop. Include three bottles marked only with colored geometric stickers, a
sensor probe, and an abstract evidence timeline with no readable text. Use
overcast daylight, moss green, slate, teal, and amber. No logos, trademarks,
watermark, medical claims, or dramatic pollution imagery.

## Thermal chamber counterexample

An editorial 16:9 photograph of a fictional robotics engineer stress-testing a
sensor module in a small thermal chamber and investigating a red outlier on an
abstract laptop evidence graph. Include credible electronics tools and ordinary
lab imperfections. Use cool practical lighting with a warm task lamp. No logos,
readable text, watermark, holograms, dangerous sparks, or stock-photo posing.

## Team evidence review

An editorial 16:9 photograph of three fictional researchers reviewing
conflicting evidence. Show a screen and corkboard with abstract colored
node-and-edge graphs, one highlighted red counterexample, printed plots, and
serious collaborative body language. Use soft daylight and restrained teal,
amber, red, and green accents. No logos, readable text, watermark, exaggerated
reactions, holograms, or high-fives.
